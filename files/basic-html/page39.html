                                    39 IVEX PROSPERITY THROUGH PEOPLEComparison of Cleaning Process TimesBefore & After SMED ImprovementSMED Case Study: Reduce Downtime Gas Turbine in Replacement Air Intake Filter at Indorama Ventures Indonesia Reducing downtime during critical maintenance tasks is essential for maximizing operational efficiency. In 2022, Indorama Ventures Indonesia in Tangerang, Indonesia, launched an improvement initiative to reduce gas turbine (GT) downtime during the replacement of the air intake filter stage #3 by applying Single-Minute Exchange of Die (SMED) principles.The air intake filter requires replacement when the differential pressure indicator shows over >0.500 kPa, requiring the GT to be shut down and impacting fiber production. The replacement process involves both external and internal setup activities. However, the most time-consuming aspect proved to be the GT cooling down process, which allows restart only below 90%u00b0C. Traditionally, an automatic ventilating fan assists in cooling, but it operates for only 30 minutes. The improvement involved running the ventilation fan in manual mode until the GT temperature dropped below 90%u00b0C. This adjustment accelerated the cooling down process and reduced downtime. The project achieved significant results, cutting GT downtime by 60 minutes per cycle, from 4.5 to 3.5 hours. This led to reduced operational costs, improved quality, and increased productivity. The project highlights the effectiveness of applying SMED principles to streamline production processes and enhance operational excellence, even when a complete conversion from internal to external setup is not possible.
