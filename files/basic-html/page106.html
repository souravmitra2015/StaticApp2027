                                    106 IVEX PROSPERITY THROUGH PEOPLEKey inefficiencies identified from the Fishbone Diagram (Ishikawa Analysis) and Pareto Analysis revealed four critical root causes:1. Dew point fluctuations exceeding process limits, causing unstable IV growth and drying inefficiencies.2. Incomplete regeneration cycles due to inefficient heating, frequently shortened from 8 to 6-7 hours, reducing moisture removal.3. Insufficient condensate drainage, leading to moisture retention and reabsorption in the regenerated bed.4. High regeneration blower suction pressure, preventing proper condensate removal and reducing drying efficiency.Further inferential analysis validated that excessive blower suction pressure retained condensed water, leading to moisture reabsorption in the regenerated bed and further destabilizing IV consistency. A Cause-and-Effect Matrix was used to prioritize these critical issues based on their impact. These findings emphasized the need for targeted process modifications and equipment upgrades, which were systematically addressed in the Improve Phase.IMPROVE %u2022 Why-Why Analysis%u2022 Design of Experiments (DOE)%u2022 Process Flow Diagram (PFD)%u2022 FMEA (Failure Modes and Effects Analysis)%u2022 Pilot Testing & Validation%u2022 Regression Analysis%u2022 SPC (Statistical Process Control) ChartsDMAIC TOOLS APPLIEDThe Improve Phase focused on engineering modifications and process optimizations to stabilize dew point, enhance regeneration efficiency, and optimize blower performance. A Why-Why Analysis confirmed that equipment design issues and process inefficiencies were the main constraints. To validate and implement improvements systematically, a Design of Experiments (DOE) approach was used to test different solutions before full-scale deployment.The team implemented several improvements, including:%u2022 Installation of a high-capacity cooler to improve heat exchange efficiency, validated through pilot testing and regression analysis to measure its impact on temperature stability.%u2022 Condensate removal demister installation to eliminate moisture retention, supported by Failure Modes and Effects Analysis (FMEA) to assess potential risks.%u2022 Relocation of the old cooler with optimized piping layouts, guided by Process Flow Diagrams (PFDs) to ensure smooth integration into the system.Implementation of demister level switches with logic control valves to regulate condensate drainage, monitored using Statistical Process Control (SPC) charts for real-time performance tracking.These modifications extended the regeneration cycle from 6-7 hours to 11.5 hours, ensuring complete moisture removal and stabilizing IV growth. The results demonstrated a significant reduction in process variation, an increase in SSP throughput, and an improvement in process capability (Cpk from 0.63 to 1.45), confirming the effectiveness of the implemented solutions.
