                                    17 IVEX PROSPERITY THROUGH PEOPLETakt Time is a crucial metric in Lean Manufacturing, reflecting the Available Time (total time available for production excluding breaks or maintenance periods) divided by the Customer Demand. It establishes the rate at which a product must be manufactured in order to meet customer demands while avoiding overproduction.Takt TimeManufacturingTime to ProduceCustomer DemandRate of SalesTakt Time Key guideline to calculate Takt Time as follows:%u2022 Determine Available Production Time: Identify the total amount of time available for production within a specific period, which is commonly stated in hours or minutes. Subtract any scheduled pauses or downtime.%u2022 Define Customer Demand: The quantity of products or services needed within a specified timeframe, often expressed in units or items per period (e.g., per day, hour, or shift). Accurate demand assessment is crucial for planning, scheduling, and calculating Takt Time, ensuring manufacturing aligns with customer needs for efficient and synchronized production.%u2022 Calculate Takt Time: Takt Time is calculated as the ratio of available production time to customer demand.Key guideline to implement Takt Time as follows:To effectively implement Takt Time in the production process, the following actions should be taken:Clearly Communicate Takt Time: Conduct raining sessions to ensure that everyone comprehend the concept and its importance in achieving the production objective.Provide Visual Aids in the Production Area such as:- Andon System, incorporating Takt Time information, provides immediate production feedback. This aids teams in quickly identifying and addressing issues, ensuring timely problem resolution.- Takt Time Boards or Production Control Boards displaying key production information, including Takt Time, Cycle times, and Actual production rates, facilitate real-time monitoring and adjustment of production processes.- Discuss Adjustments to Takt Time on a regular basis in meetings as customer demand changes, into a PDCA circle - Integrate Takt Time into KPIs to underline its impact on overall production efficiency.Manufacturing bottlenecks are process steps that limit output by blocking workflow, generating lack of productivity. De-bottlenecking or fixing bottlenecks boosts efficiency and prevents delays in manufacturing operation. Equipment upgrades, process optimization, and resource reallocation help reduce bottlenecks and boost production. Productivity increases with faster production, better resource use, and shorter lead times.
