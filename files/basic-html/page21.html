                                    21 IVEX PROSPERITY THROUGH PEOPLEDEMURRAGE COSTS 2021Case Study: Demurrage Cost Reduction through Value Stream Mapping (VSM) at Indovinya Mau%u00e1In the petrochemical and chemical industries, Demurrage costs%u2014penalties for delays in loading or unloading cargo - can significantly impact operations in the petrochemical and chemical industries, where handling hazardous materials under strict safety regulations is critical. In 2021, Indovinya Mau%u00e1 incurred $106,000 in such fees, prompting a project to streamline internal processes, reduce delays, and set a new standard for operational efficiency. The initiative aimed to cut demurrage costs by 40% by optimizing bulk and packaged material handling within Mau%u00e1%u2019s Chemical and Petrochemical units, focusing solely on internal operations. Lean tools and the PDCA (Plan-Do-Check-Act) methodology were employed to identify inefficiencies, implement targeted improvements, and ensure sustainable results. To accomplish these objectives, the project adopted Lean tools in conjunction with the PDCA (Plan-Do-CheckAct) methodology, focusing on identifying inefficiencies, implementing targeted improvements, and ensuring the sustainability of the enhancements over time.Implementation Steps The project began with a thorough Value Stream Mapping (VSM) exercise to analyze the existing loading and unloading processes in detail. This step helped identify bottlenecks and inefficiencies, forming the foundation for creating an optimized process flow.To address the identified issues, a variety of Lean tools were applied. The Ishikawa Diagram was used to pinpoint root causes of delays, while 5W1H helped define key aspects of the problems and their solutions. Kaizenprinciples were employed to drive continuous improvements, supported by 6S practices to enhance workplace organization and safety. Additionally, Heijunka leveled workloads for better resource allocation, Jidoka integrated automation to improve process monitoring, and SMED techniques reduced setup times to boost overall efficiency.An action plan comprising 17 targeted measures was executed between 2022 and 2023.
