                                    80 IVEX PROSPERITY THROUGH PEOPLEThe Project Charter provided a comprehensive framework, outlining essential components such as the business case, problem/opportunity statement, goal statement, project scope, champion, team members, project lead, project status, stakeholders, and primary and secondary metrics. Both the Voice of Business and Voice of Process played a pivotal role in pinpointing critical business and process requirements, aiming to reduce natural gas consumption, costs, and greenhouse gas emissions, while optimizing the Heating Transfer Medium (HTM) system and the esterification reactor without jeopardizing the process.The SIPOC Diagram visually mapped the suppliers, inputs, process, outputs, and customers of the HTM system and the esterification reactor. The Flow Diagram of the HTM Users Process provided detailed information to identify potential factors to highlight opportunities for reducing natural gas consumption. Additionally, the Fishbone Diagram systematically categorized potential causes of high natural gas consumption into six main categories: process, equipment, people, material, environment, and method.
