                                    38 IVEX PROSPERITY THROUGH PEOPLESMED Case Study: IPET Time Reduction of Cleaning SSP 3&4 Lines at Indorama Ventures Polymers MexicoThe cleaning process of SPP 3&4 lines in Indorama Ventures Polymers Mexico (IVPM) is carried out every four months to ensure an efficient heat exchange in the equipment and to guarantee the design capacity and quality. The typical cleaning time for the two lines is five days, equivalent to 76.8 hours of production loss and 1,109 tons on average (data collected in 2019-2022). The objective of this project is to reduce the cleaning time by 25% and create a profit of 831 tons/year. Separation of External Elements: The pre-crystallizer and stabilizer cleaning processes were streamlined. The equipment emptying procedure, previously integrated with the cooling process, was separated.Conversion and Removal: The aperture temperature was adjusted to reduce waiting time (from 50 to 150%u00b0C). New fans were installed to enhance cooling within the equipment and new windows were added to the building to improve airflow, reducing waiting time for cooling.Streamlining Processes:%u2022 The second air buffer tanks were installed to stabilize the air supply, and the vacuum system was enhanced to expedite equipment preparation.%u2022 Cleaning resources were doubled, a lifting system was added to handle heavy components, and pneumatic tools were acquired to facilitate quicker opening and closing of taps.%u2022 Rotary relay valves were pre-positioned for easier access, and a crane was installed to accelerate pre-shutdown operations.The project achieved a 40.6% reduction in cleaning time, surpassing the initial goal of 25% and resulting in production gains of 831 tons per year, along with financial savings of approximately $366,000 USD. Detailed metrics showed a reduction in production loss from 1,109 tons to 709 tons per cleaning event, yielding a net profit of $92,630 to $145,231 USD per event. This success underscores the effectiveness of the implemented improvements and the dedicated efforts of the team.SMED Application and Redefined Critical Path
