                                    189 IVEX PROSPERITY THROUGH PEOPLEThe Plan phase focused on minimizing utility expenses%u2014particularly in steam and cooling water consumption - without compromising operational demand. The team conducted a detailed cost analysis to identify inefficiencies and areas for improvement. Rather than investing in capital-intensive solutions, the project prioritized process optimization to reduce costs and eliminate penalties for exceeding contracted demand limits.The findings revealed that steam accounted for 32% of total utility costs, with the Sulfation Plant consuming 70% of that amount. Cooling water represented 12% of total expenses, with 82% of AGR cooling water used in the Sulfation Plant. Key contributors to excessive consumption included steam leaks, outdated equipment, inefficient process control, and inadequate monitoring systems.A Gemba Walk further highlighted 17 defective steam traps and 10 steam leakage points, which were major sources of unnecessary energy waste.
