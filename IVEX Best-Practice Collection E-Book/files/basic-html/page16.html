                                    16 IVEX PROSPERITY THROUGH PEOPLE2) Value & Waste Philosophy Voice of Customer (VOC) & ValueThe Value and Waste Philosophy focuses on maximizing customer value while minimizing waste. This philosophy is divided into two key dimensions: VOC and Valueand VSM and Muda.The Voice of Customers (VOC) serves as the foundation for delivering quality and value within an organization. By actively gathering Internal & External customer input-such as feedback, survey data, and service expectations, businesses can translate these insights into product specifications that clearly define what constitutes value and quality. Ignoring VOC can lead to customer dissatisfaction and missed opportunities for improvement.Key areas for assessment in VOC dimension include understanding and meeting both internal and external customer requirements. This ensures that the needs of all stakeholders are addressed effectively. Additionally, implementing formal customer satisfaction systems for both internal and external customers is critical for continuously measuring and improving service quality.
