                                    185 IVEX PROSPERITY THROUGH PEOPLEThe project began with a detailed energy audit to identify inefficiencies within the utility pump system. Analysis revealed that 41% of the plant%u2019s total energy consumption was attributed to utilities, with three cooling water pumps consuming 2,300 kWh daily, even during low machine operation. The CP3 and CP4 pumps were identified as major contributors to energy waste due to their low efficiency (~30%) and extended operational hours. To address this, the plan aimed to replace these pumps with a high-efficiency KBL DB 65-20xe (18.5 kW) model, ensuring significant power savings and enhanced system performance.
