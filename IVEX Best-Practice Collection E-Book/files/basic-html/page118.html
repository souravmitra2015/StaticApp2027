                                    118 IVEX PROSPERITY THROUGH PEOPLEThe Define phase establishes the foundation of the project by outlining the problem, objectives, and scope. With rising energy costs, the company faces increasing financial pressure and challenges in meeting its sustainability goals. Reducing energy consumption will enhance efficiency, lower operational expenses, and support sustainability commitments. In 2023, the average monthly energy consumption reached 731 kWh/MT, exceeding the target of 705 kWh/MT, leading to higher costs and reduced efficiency. To address this, the project aims to reduce energy consumption to 680 kWh/MT, with an estimated savings of USD 82,842.Excessive energy consumption contributes to inefficiencies and increased costs, negatively affecting both financial performance and environmental sustainability. The project%u2019s primary goal is to achieve targeted reductions in key energy-consuming systems, specifically reducing consumption in the Drying System (164.45 kWh/MT), Air Compressor System (25.65 kWh/MT), and Lighting System (35.71 kWh/MT). The project scope focuses on optimizing the dryer and cooling systems, which are major contributors to overall energy use.DEFINE DMAIC %u2022 Project CharterTOOLS APPLIEDThe Measure phase focuses on collecting and analyzing energy consumption data to establish a baseline and identify areas for improvement. Energy data from 2023 showed significant variations across months and equipment types. The highest energy consumption was recorded in January (800 KWH/MT), while the lowest was in August (670 KWH/MT). Machine utilization accounted for the largest share, averaging 274 KWH/MT, followed by the Dryer + Dehumidifier (186 KWH/MT) and the Cooling System (184 KWH/MT).
