                                    93 IVEX PROSPERITY THROUGH PEOPLEThe qualitative analysis began with a presentation of the collected data to the shift leaders, with the goal of determining the underlying causes of the observed high variability and long processing times. Key findings included concurrent line shutdowns, dispersed task execution across multiple machines, which was frequently managed by small groups or individual workers, and instances of operating staff waiting gaps as a consequence of a lack of precise instructions from shift leaders.Further analysis included categorizing setup-time activities as internal or external. The time spent on each activity was measured, and the number of times each activity was completed in a shift was reported in order to calculate the total time required based on the number of shifts and workforce. Three types of setups were selected for analysis due to their frequent deployment. In addition, critical issues contributing to misaligned times were identified, such as malfunctioning knotting guns, material shortages such as air guns for cleaning, dewebbers, spare combs, and gluing tape, misplaced adhesive tanks and other materials that hindered dewebber change, as well as time wasting on loading of empty tubes and unloading of tubes with tails.During this stage, the project team developed and implemented solutions to address the underlying causes of the lengthy and inconsistent setup times.
